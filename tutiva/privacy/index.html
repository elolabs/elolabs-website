Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your lessons, students and payments stay on your phone. Tutiva has no accounts and no server, and we never receive your lesson or student data. The only thing that goes over the internet is your subscription purchase.

1. Who we are

Tutiva is an app made by EloLabs for private tutors and teachers. In this policy, "you" means the teacher who uses the app, and "students and parents" means the people you record in it.

2. The app on your device

Everything you enter in Tutiva is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. This includes:

  • Students (name, phone number, level, school, notes) and parent contact details
  • Lesson plans, lessons, attendance, topics, homework and lesson notes
  • Fees, packages, payments and balances
  • Holidays and your app settings

There is no account and no sign-in, and the app works without an internet connection. EloLabs does not receive, see or store any of this information.

3. When the app uses the internet

Tutiva uses the internet only for the optional Tutiva Pro subscription. Purchases are made through the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat processes your purchase history together with an anonymous app user identifier created by the app.

  • We never see your payment details. Payments are handled by Apple or Google.
  • No lesson, student or payment-record data from the app is sent to RevenueCat or to us.
  • The anonymous identifier is not linked to your name or email.

4. Things you share yourself

  • Lesson summaries and payment reminders: when you choose to send one, Tutiva opens WhatsApp or your phone's SMS app with the message already written. You decide whether to send it. The message goes from your phone, through that app; we never see it.
  • Progress reports (PDF) and backup files: these are created on your phone and shared through your phone's share menu to the place you choose.
  • Notifications: lesson reminders and backup reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No data leaves your device to make them work.

5. Backups

You can create a backup file at any time (Settings → Backup) and keep it wherever you like. If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), Tutiva's data may be included in it. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms; we have no access to them.

6. Free usage counter

Tutiva is free for your first 100 lessons. To count them, the app stores the date you started using it. This date is also saved in your backup files. On iPhone, it is additionally stored in the device Keychain, on this device only, so that the free allowance can't be restarted by reinstalling the app.

  • It is only a date. It contains no personal, student or lesson information and never leaves your phone.
  • "Delete all data" does not remove it, and on iPhone it may remain after the app is deleted.

7. Students' and parents' information

You may record information about your students and their parents, including minors. This information stays on your phone and is under your control. Please record only what you need for your lessons. If a student or parent asks you to remove their information, you can delete the student in the app (Student → Info → Delete).

8. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store and Google Play: app distribution and payments.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management (see section 3).
  • WhatsApp and your SMS app: only when you open them to send a message.
  • Web3Forms: only for the contact form on our support website, not in the app.

Tutiva contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

9. Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you delete it yourself: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. See Deleting your data for details, including what remains and how to ask for your RevenueCat purchase record to be deleted.

10. Children's privacy

Tutiva is a tool for teachers and is not directed at children. Information about students, including children, is recorded by the teacher and stays on the teacher's device. EloLabs does not collect it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at support@elolabs.app.
