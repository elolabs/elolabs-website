What You Get
Everything a tutor tracks. In one place.
📅
An agenda that fills itself

Set a student's plan once, every Tuesday at 17:00 for example, and Tutiva puts every lesson on your agenda. Mark each lesson with one tap when it's done.

  • Weekly, every two weeks, monthly or one-off lessons
  • Done, cancelled, no-show or moved, all in one tap
  • Day strip and month calendar showing how busy each day is
  • Holidays marked on the calendar
  • Lesson reminders before each lesson
📈
See every student's progress

Keep a topic list for each subject, note what you covered and what comes next, and give homework. Tutiva shows how far each student has come.

  • Topics covered and the next topic, lesson by lesson
  • Homework with done, partial or not done
  • Progress report as a PDF to share with parents
💳
Know who still owes you

Charge per lesson, sell lesson packages, bill monthly or teach for free. Record payments, including partial ones and advances, and see your balance at a glance.

  • Per lesson, packages, monthly fee or free
  • Partial payments and advances settle automatically
  • Pending amounts per student, totals by week, month and year
  • Hide amounts with one tap when others can see your screen
💬
Keep parents in the loop

After a lesson, send a short summary to the parent or student: the topic covered, the next topic and the homework. Payment reminders are just as quick. Tutiva opens WhatsApp or your SMS app with the message ready; you press send.

  • Lesson summary by WhatsApp or SMS
  • Polite payment reminders with the lesson dates
  • Nothing is sent without you
